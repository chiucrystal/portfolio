My role

I conducted research and designed the new tool end-to-end alongside another designer, from research through handoff. We delivered a single tool that meets basic authoring expectations, and a PrimeVue component library that was introduced across the broader admin platform.

Why now?

The project started as a technical migration. Design was brought in early to modernise the UI and make small usability improvements.

Discovery

We conducted contextual interviews with power users to better understand the existing workflow. The conversations revealed a process that spanned multiple platforms and was much bigger than just an UI problem.

Before content reached CCA, editors had already touched four other tools.

Hover (or tap) a bubble to see what was happening there.

It was clear that the process was made up of workarounds as the platform no longer supported how authors drafted and edited.

  • The interface didn't support basic editing tasks directly. Adding, reordering, or configuring content required navigating outside normal interaction patterns or into source code.
  • Previewing content required making the page live or editing via HTML and CSS, making the process high risk and technically demanding.
The problem reframed:

How might we redesign the course authoring experience so authors can build and edit content quickly, without needing technical knowledge?

What we tested

Two concepts were brought into testing:

Manage content where it's created to reduce process fragmentation

Widget and page properties can be edited contextually within the tool.

Live preview of content before publishing to reduce the risk of the editing process and encourage trial and error

Diagram/asset placeholder
What we found
  • The core direction was validated during testing. Creators adapted quickly and responded positively to the presented concepts.
What was shipped

The three concepts combined to bring the entire iterative creation process into one tool. Creators no longer needed multiple tools to draft, edit, and preview.

Some key features included:

  • Configuration moved to a panel next to the canvas
  • Display settings simplified to one level
  • Draft and preview state added for most interactive widgets

Results and impact
  • Defined and implemented a new component library to be used in internal tools.
  • Positive reception from 11 internal users at launch.
  • 6 new widgets have been requested and integrated since launch, suggesting creators are working within the tool rather than around it.
